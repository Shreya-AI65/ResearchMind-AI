# Problem Statement

## Project Title

**ResearchMind AI: A Multi-Agent Research Intelligence Platform for Scientific Literature Analysis**

## Background

The rapid growth of Artificial Intelligence research has resulted in thousands of research papers being published every month across journals, conferences, and preprint servers. Researchers, students, and AI engineers often spend a significant amount of time searching for relevant papers, reading abstracts, comparing methodologies, identifying benchmark datasets, and understanding current research trends before starting a new project. This manual process is time-consuming, repetitive, and often leads to information overload.

Although several AI-powered research assistants exist today, most of them primarily focus on summarizing individual papers or answering questions from uploaded documents. They provide limited support for comparing multiple research papers, identifying research gaps, analyzing benchmark results, recommending future research directions, or assisting in experiment planning.

## Problem Statement

There is a need for an intelligent research assistant that can go beyond simple summarization and help users analyze an entire research domain. Such a system should automatically retrieve relevant research papers, extract important information, compare different approaches, identify common strengths and limitations, highlight research gaps, and generate structured literature reviews. It should also assist researchers in planning future experiments based on insights gathered from existing work.

## Proposed Solution

ResearchMind AI is a multi-agent research intelligence platform that automates the literature review process using specialized AI agents. Each agent performs a dedicated task such as research planning, paper retrieval, information extraction, comparative analysis, research-gap identification, and report generation. By combining these agents into a coordinated workflow, the platform aims to reduce the time required for literature review while helping researchers make informed decisions and discover potential research opportunities.

## Target Users

* Undergraduate and postgraduate students
* PhD researchers
* Professors and academic researchers
* AI engineers
* Startup research and development teams
* Anyone conducting technical literature reviews

## Expected Impact

The proposed system aims to significantly reduce the effort involved in understanding a new research area by providing structured insights instead of isolated summaries. It will help users quickly identify state-of-the-art methods, benchmark datasets, existing limitations, and future research opportunities, enabling faster and more informed research decisions.


## Existing Solutions

Several AI-powered research assistants, including NotebookLM, SciSpace, Elicit, and Consensus, help users search, summarize, and understand research papers. These tools significantly reduce the time required for literature review and document comprehension by providing AI-assisted summaries, explanations, and source-grounded responses.

However, these platforms primarily focus on document understanding rather than supporting the complete research workflow.


## Research Gap

Although current AI research assistants effectively support literature understanding, researchers still spend significant time on higher-level research activities such as comparing methodologies across multiple papers, identifying research gaps, evaluating experimental approaches, planning experiments, selecting datasets and evaluation metrics, and synthesizing knowledge from diverse sources.

This project aims to address these challenges by developing **ResearchMind AI**, an Agentic AI-based research collaborator that employs multiple specialized AI agents to support the complete research lifecycle. Instead of functioning solely as a document summarization tool, the proposed system will assist users in literature analysis, research-gap discovery, experiment planning, and research decision-making through collaborative AI agents.
