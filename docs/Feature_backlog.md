# Feature Backlog

## Project Name

**ResearchMind AI – A Multi-Agent Research Intelligence Platform**

---

# Version 1 (Minimum Viable Product - MVP)

The goal of Version 1 is to build a functional platform that assists researchers in performing literature reviews efficiently using a multi-agent AI workflow.

## 1. User Module

### Features

* Enter a research topic or query.
* View previous research sessions.
* Save research history.

---

## 2. Research Paper Retrieval

### Features

* Search for relevant research papers.
* Retrieve paper metadata (Title, Authors, Abstract, Publication Year).
* Filter search results based on keywords.
* Display paper summaries.

---

## 3. Research Paper Analysis

### Features

* Generate concise summaries.
* Extract research objectives.
* Extract methodologies.
* Extract datasets used.
* Extract evaluation metrics.
* Identify strengths.
* Identify limitations.

---

## 4. Paper Comparison

### Features

* Compare multiple research papers.
* Generate comparison tables.
* Highlight similarities.
* Highlight differences.
* Compare benchmark results.

---

## 5. Research Intelligence

### Features

* Detect common research gaps.
* Identify future research opportunities.
* Recommend benchmark datasets.
* Suggest potential research directions.

---

## 6. Literature Review Generation

### Features

* Generate structured literature reviews.
* Organize content by research themes.
* Export reports as Markdown.
* Export reports as PDF.
