# Success Criteria

ResearchMind AI will be considered successful if it can:

* Retrieve high-quality research papers.
* Generate structured summaries.
* Compare methodologies across papers.
* Detect research gaps.
* Assist in experiment planning.
* Produce complete research reports.
* Reduce manual effort in scientific research.

---

# Long-Term Goal

To build a collaborative AI-powered research assistant that combines semantic search, intelligent reasoning, multi-agent collaboration, and scientific workflow automation into a single unified platform for researchers worldwide.
